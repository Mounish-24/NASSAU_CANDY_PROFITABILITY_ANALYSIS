# Executive Summary: Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

**Target Audience**: Executive Management & Business Leadership  
**Company**: Nassau Candy Distributor  
**Project Date**: September 2026  

---

## 1. Context & Business Problem
Nassau Candy Distributor operates across multiple product lines (Sugar, Chocolate, and Other categories) distributing nationwide. Previously, leadership lacked unified clarity into whether high sales revenue translated directly into gross profit and where cost-related margin risks were present.

---

## 2. Key Findings Summary

```text
========================================================================
METRIC                             VALUE
========================================================================
Total Revenue                      $485,739.06
Total Cost                         $316,211.52
Total Gross Profit                 $169,527.54
Overall Gross Margin               34.90%
Total Units Sold                   28,142 units
Cleaned Orders Analyzed            2,500 orders
Active Product Catalog             15 products
========================================================================
```

1. **Division Margin Stability**:
   - **Sugar Division**: Revenue of **$218,415.62**, Gross Profit of **$76,585.22** (Gross Margin: **35.06%**)
   - **Chocolate Division**: Revenue of **$188,712.18**, Gross Profit of **$66,772.07** (Gross Margin: **35.38%**)
   - **Other Division**: Revenue of **$78,611.26**, Gross Profit of **$26,170.25** (Gross Margin: **33.29%**)

2. **Top Product Profit Drivers**:
   - `SweeTARTS` generated the highest absolute profit (**$31,240.10** across 255 orders).
   - `Everlasting Gobstopper` generated **$24,115.42** in profit.
   - `Wonka Bar - Fudge Mallows` generated **$18,920.15** in profit.

3. **Pareto (80/20) Concentration Analysis**:
   - Out of 15 products, **12 products (80.0%)** generate 80% of company revenue and gross profit.
   - The catalog demonstrates healthy diversification without excessive single-SKU reliance.

---

## 3. Key Recommendations for Action

1. **Expand Supply Allocation for Top SKUs**: Ensure zero stockouts for `SweeTARTS` and `Everlasting Gobstopper`, which generate over 32% of total business profit.
2. **Standardize Pricing Policies in 'Other' Division**: Align markup parameters for specialty non-chocolate/sugar items to achieve the company target 35% margin.
3. **Deploy Streamlit BI Dashboard**: Embed the newly created dashboard into monthly management review workflows for real-time risk tracking.
